# Hyperion icon concept v1

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Concept asset for design review; not yet wired into application packaging.

Direction: an H joined by an orbital ribbon, connecting Hyperion's existing orbital imagery with its role as a personal knowledge base. Indigo follows the current interface accent.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: standalone desktop app icon for Hyperion, a local-first personal knowledge base for connected notes, ideas and meeting transcriptions.
Primary request: Design one refined, distinctive, professional app icon. Evolve the idea of connected knowledge and orbital paths into a bold memorable abstract H monogram: two substantial upright softly rounded forms joined by a single elegant curved connecting band, suggesting an orbital connection and pages of knowledge. The silhouette should immediately read as a cohesive H with a little character, simple enough to reproduce as a single-color logo.
Style: restrained modern desktop app identity, precise vector-like geometry with a subtly dimensional satin finish. Calm, thoughtful, confident.
Color palette: the app's indigo accent #4b65d1, lighter periwinkle highlights, deep midnight indigo tile, near-white lavender symbol. Use controlled subtle tonal gradients, excellent contrast.
Composition: square 1024x1024 canvas, one centered rounded-square app tile occupying 88% of canvas, with transparent outside corners/background. Large central H symbol with generous balanced internal padding. Straight-on orthographic view.
Constraints: actual transparent background outside tile; finished isolated icon asset, no presentation board, no lettering or wordmark, no captions, no extra variations. Strong legibility at 32 pixels. No tiny nodes, no fine orbital lines, no stars, no brain, no lightbulb, no generic AI sparkle, no elaborate network, no photographic texture, no mockup scene, no extraneous ornament, no watermark.
